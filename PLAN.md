## Summary

Open tooling and methods for tracing claims back to primary sources — surfacing provenance so readers can judge for themselves, explicitly without acting as an arbiter of truth.

## Scope

In scope: documented methods to link claims to primary sources and record provenance. Out of scope: adjudicating truth/falsity, rating sources, or partisan labeling.

## Risk

Medium: educational/derivative work on openly-licensed sources with recorded provenance; no personal data and no prescriptive/personalized advice. Each deliverable cites sources and stays within its stated non-advice / non-arbiter / non-partisan bounds.

## Approach

A sourcing framework (not-arbiter), a primary-source-linking guide, a provenance record format, evaluation/limits, and a worked example.

## Definition of shipped

Documented, open tooling and methods for linking claims to primary sources (not adjudicating truth), adopted by a newsroom or research group.
